What is Budgetum Desktop Analytics?

A free companion to the Budgetum budget planner app. Export your transactions from your phone and explore them on a big screen: breakdowns, trends, a spending calendar, recurring payments, and net worth. No account and no sign-in.

A plain CSV export works too. The ZIP with analytics data adds your categories, accounts, saved sets, and recurring schedules, so the reports match the app.

How to export from the app

  1. 1In the Budgetum app, open the More tab and tap Import / Export.
  2. 2Pick the period: All time, Month, Year, or Custom period.
  3. 3Tick Include analytics data.
  4. 4Tap Export CSV and save the ZIP file to your computer.
  5. 5Drop the ZIP on this page, or click the upload area to choose it.

Reports you get

  • Overview

    Income, expenses, and what remains for any period.

  • Money flow

    How income flows into sections and categories.

  • Breakdown

    Spending or income by section, category, contact, tag, or account.

  • Trends

    Days, weeks, months, quarters, or years, with comparisons.

  • Spending calendar

    Daily heatmap, year view, and weekday patterns.

  • Categories

    Each category with its trend, payees, tags, and accounts.

  • Contacts

    Top payees, contact types, and recurring patterns.

  • Tags

    Tag coverage, combinations, and trends.

  • Sets

    Your saved sets from the app, with totals and reports.

  • Recurring

    Recurring templates, upcoming dates, and history.

  • Capital

    Net worth across accounts, assets, and liabilities over time.

  • Transactions

    Every transaction with search and filters.

Private by design

  • Your file is read locally in this browser. Transactions are never uploaded.
  • The last import is kept in this browser so you can come back to it. Use Clear & upload to remove it.
  • If your accounts use more than one currency, only the currency codes are sent to fetch exchange rates.
